Auto mode & staying safe

Who is responsible

clipped is a tool that runs on your computer with your accounts. You are responsible for:

  • having permission to repost each streamer's clips. Many streamers welcome clip channels; some don't. Ask, and keep a list.
  • following the Twitch Terms of Service (https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/terms-of-service/) and the YouTube Community Guidelines and policies, including the rules on reused content for monetisation (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392).
  • responding to takedown requests from creators.

The first time you switch anything to Auto mode, clipped asks you to confirm this.

Built-in guard rails

  • Manual approval is the default. Nothing uploads until you approve it.
  • Approved streamers only (on by default in Auto mode): clips from streamers who aren't on your approved list, or on that category's allowlist, go to the review queue instead of being uploaded.
  • Rate limits: a minimum gap between any two uploads (default 20 minutes), a per-day cap, your chosen interval, quiet hours, and a "pause until" switch.
  • Quota aware: clipped stops before YouTube's daily API quota runs out and resumes after the reset.
  • Credit is mandatory: every title or description, including your manual edits, keeps the streamer's name, a link to their channel, and a link to the original clip.
  • No near-duplicates: the same clip is never processed twice, and clips of the same moment or too similar to recent uploads are skipped.

Make uploads more than a repost

YouTube treats unedited re-uploads as reused content. clipped's edits help: captions, the reframe, hook text, dead-air trimming and the flash-forward. Adding your own voice helps even more:

  • the commentary line (on screen and in the description), editable per clip in the review queue
  • an intro or outro clip (Settings → Layout), such as a 1-2 second branded sting or your own reaction
  • picking clips with a clear story and writing your own title

Recommended settings for a new channel

  • Stay in Manual mode for the first week.
  • 2-4 uploads per day, interval of 2 hours or more, quiet hours overnight.
  • Only add streamers you've asked to your approved list.
